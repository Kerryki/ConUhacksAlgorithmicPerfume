# Project Overview – AI Perfume Lab

## Project Title

**AI Perfume Lab – Personalized Fragrance Creation Powered by AI**

---

## Project Summary

AI Perfume Lab is an immersive, AI-driven platform that enables users to design a **fully personalized fragrance** through a visually rich, gamified journey. Instead of traditional questionnaires, the system captures user identity, mood, and preferences through **interactive UI pages**, then translates those inputs into a custom perfume formulation and bottle design using machine-learning models.

The experience blends **luxury aesthetics, emotional storytelling, and algorithmic design**, positioning perfume creation as both an art and a science.

---

## Core Goals

- Translate human identity and emotion into fragrance using AI
- Replace forms with **intuitive, playful interactions**
- Create a premium, memorable user journey
- Generate a complete perfume output: name, bottle, and composition

---

## Target Audience

- Perfume enthusiasts and luxury consumers
- Users interested in AI-powered creativity
- Individuals seeking self-expression through scent

---

## Complete User Flow Overview

The experience is structured into **three foundational identity pages**, followed by **refinement pages**, and ending with a **final AI-generated result**.

---

## FOUNDATIONAL INPUT PAGES

### 1. Intro Page – AI Perfume Lab

**Purpose:** Set tone, brand identity, and intrigue.

**Design:**

- Dark, luxury black mode
- Subtle glowing particles and refined typography

**Content:**

- Title: _AI Perfume Lab_
- Subtitle: _Scent by Algorithm_
- Tagline: _Design your signature fragrance through our AI-powered scent synthesis protocol_

**Action:**

- Primary CTA: **Start Design → Algorithmic Fragrance Design**

**Outcome:**

- Begins the fragrance creation journey

---

### 2. Personality Page – Core Identity Selection

**Purpose:** Capture the user’s core personality, which acts as the emotional anchor for scent generation.

**Layout Concept:**

- Background: staggered **hexagonal grid** inspired by honeycombs and chemical charts
- Foreground: **3D deck of oversized vertical cards**, fanned out

**Seven Core Personalities:**

1. The Optimist
2. The Romantic
3. The Minimalist
4. The Explorer
5. The Rebel
6. The Intellectual
7. The Enigma

**Interaction:**

- Users swipe horizontally through cards
- Cards react with depth, tilt, and shadow
- Selecting a card brings it forward
- Selected card flips to reveal:

  - Associated scent families
  - A **Compatibility Score**

**Compatibility Hook:**

- Generated via a quick **2-question micro-quiz**
- Reinforces personalization without friction

**Outcome:**

- Personality becomes the primary emotional driver in AI scent synthesis

---

### 3. Age Page – Maturity & Intensity Mapping

**Purpose:** Adjust scent strength and balance based on life stage.

**User Choices:**

- Children / Teens (10–18)
- Young Adults (19–25)
- Adults (25–50)
- Mature & Beyond (50+)

**Outcome:**

- Influences concentration, sharpness, and depth of notes

---

### 4. Color Picker Page – Emotional & Visual Preference

**Purpose:** Capture emotional tone and aesthetic taste.

**Interaction:**

- Circular color wheel
- Drag interaction with magnifying-glass lens effect

**Outcome:**

- Influences bottle color, label design, and subtle scent bias

---

## REFINEMENT PAGES

### 5. Presence Page – Masculine / Feminine Expression

**Purpose:** Allow fluid gender expression in fragrance design.

**Interaction:**

- 2D gradient plane (feminine ↔ masculine, 0–100)
- Diagonal line represents unisex balance (value = 50)

**Outcome:**

- Adjusts fragrance structure and note emphasis

---

### 6. Time & Mood Page

**Purpose:** Capture when and how the scent is most often worn.

**Options:**

- Golden Hour (Dawn) – Citrus & Light Floral
- High Noon – Fresh & Aquatic
- Dusk – Balanced Day/Night
- After Midnight – Dark & Heavy Notes

**Outcome:**

- Influences brightness, warmth, and depth of the fragrance

---

### 7. Main Accords / Scent Profile Page

**Purpose:** Define dominant scent families.

**Visual Scene Includes:**

- Forest → Woody
- Flower Garden → Floral
- Fruits / Fresh Water → Fresh
- Dessert → Oriental / Gourmand

**Outcome:**

- Determines the core scent structure

---

### 8. Season & Weather Page

**Purpose:** Adapt fragrance character to climate preference.

**Interaction Options:**

- Semi-circular seasonal slider (Winter → Spring → Summer → Fall)
- Optional interactive weather scene with animations (snow, sun, bloom, leaves)

**Outcome:**

- Adjusts temperature, diffusion, and freshness

---

### 9. Longevity / Projection Page

**Purpose:** Balance lasting power with scent reach.

**Concept:**
Longevity and projection exist on an inverse scale.

**UI Elements:**

- Slider: Long-Lasting ←→ Enormous Projection
- Central clock icon (longevity)
- Wi-Fi–style icon (projection)
- Aura ring responding to projection strength

**Outcome:**

- Sets final performance characteristics

---

## FINALIZATION PAGES

### 10. Name Your Perfume Page

**Purpose:** Emotional ownership.

**Options:**

- User-defined name
- AI-generated name suggestion

---

### 11. Final AI-Generated Output Page

**Purpose:** Reveal the completed personalized fragrance.

**Includes:**

- AI-generated perfume name
- Custom bottle design with label
- Optional “Created by: [User]” text
- Full fragrance breakdown:

  - Top Notes
  - Heart Notes
  - Base Notes
  - Oil ratios / composition

- Optional printable label design

---

## Final Outcome

The user completes the experience with:

- A fully personalized perfume
- A unique bottle and label design
- A clear understanding of how their identity shaped the fragrance
- A memorable, premium AI-driven creation journey
